# COLLECTIVE-002 Independent Review Instructions

**Status:** Open volunteer review request. This is a task-validity review, not an endorsement of NEXUS.
**Recruitment issue:** https://github.com/NOVAXVIII/NEXUS_Research_Public/issues/1
**Candidate task set:** [COLLECTIVE-002 candidate task set v0.1](./COLLECTIVE-002-CANDIDATE-TASK-SET-v0.1.md)

## Purpose

Please independently assess whether the eight candidate questions, expected answers, cited sources, and scoring rules are accurate, clear, fair, and reproducible. Negative findings and recommendations to revise or remove tasks are valuable.

The candidate set is a draft. It is not frozen and must not be used for scored data collection.

## Review each item T01–T08

For every item, report:

- Answer correctness: correct / partly correct / incorrect / cannot determine.
- Source support: does the cited source directly support each material part of the answer?
- Wording: could careful readers reasonably interpret the question differently?
- Scoring: could two independent scorers apply the rubric consistently?
- Missing qualification: is a unit, date, definition, rounding rule, or caveat needed?
- Recommendation: retain / revise / replace / remove.
- Evidence: exact source URL, title, retrieval date, and relevant passage, page, table, or section.

Do not rely on the draft answer key. Inspect the cited sources directly. If a source cannot be accessed or a claim is not checked, mark it **NOT VERIFIED**.

## Cross-set assessment

Please comment on:

1. Coverage and whether the eight tasks are too narrow or repetitive.
2. Difficulty balance and whether tasks are comparable.
3. Whether one task reveals another answer.
4. Shared source-family dependence.
5. Scoring reliability and exclusion conditions.
6. Fairness and possible prior-exposure advantages.
7. Which items should be revised or removed.

## Independence and version declaration

Two reviews count as separate only if submitted independently. Different account names alone do not establish independence. Please disclose prior exposure, relevant relationships, shared work, and tools used. Do not view or coordinate with another reviewer's findings before submitting your own.

Please include this declaration, completed truthfully:

> I reviewed candidate set v0.1 at the linked version. I completed this assessment independently and did not see another reviewer's submitted findings before submitting mine. I disclosed prior exposure, relevant relationships, and assistance/tools used. My report reflects the checks I actually performed; unverified items are marked as such. This declaration describes my process; it cannot prove that my private work was free of all influence.

Do not provide private credentials, government identification, or unrelated personal information. A pseudonym is acceptable.

## How to submit

Comment on the [open recruitment issue](https://github.com/NOVAXVIII/NEXUS_Research_Public/issues/1) to express interest. The coordinator will arrange separate submissions and record the exact version reviewed. Do not post private contact details publicly.

## Important exposure limitation

This candidate set and its answer keys are publicly visible for review. Anyone who reviews these exact questions and answers must not later be described as an unexposed participant in an evaluation using these same items. Any later participant evaluation must use a separate sealed task set or participants without prior exposure.

## What review can establish

A review can provide evidence about source support, clarity, fairness, and scoring reproducibility. It does not prove NEXUS improves reasoning, prove reviewer identity or expertise, or conclusively establish absence of off-record coordination. No experiment has been run and no efficacy conclusion is available.

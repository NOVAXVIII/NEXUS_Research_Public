# NEXUS — Public Research Project

**NEXUS** is an experimental reasoning methodology for helping people and AI systems clarify questions, examine evidence, compare explanations, identify useful tests, and revise conclusions when warranted.

NEXUS is a research method—not a separate proven AI system, an autonomous agent, or a claim that AI is conscious.

## The reasoning loop

1. Clarify the question and the decision it informs.
2. Identify claims, assumptions, and unknowns.
3. Separate direct observations, established findings, plausible inferences, speculation, and unknowns.
4. Compare competing explanations.
5. Identify evidence that would distinguish them and specify what could falsify them.
6. Check source provenance, independence, uncertainty, and robustness.
7. Update conclusions and preserve corrections and failures.

## Research commitments

- Truth before confirmation; evidence before confidence.
- No invented data, sources, or confidence estimates.
- Simulations are labeled as simulations, not empirical observations.
- Observations are separated from interpretations.
- Repeated claims are not counted as independent evidence without tracing their origins.
- Negative, mixed, and inconclusive results are retained.
- New experiments must justify their cost and answer a decision-relevant unresolved question.
- Respect participant consent, privacy, platform rules, and data minimization.

## Current scope

This public repository is a curated companion to a private working archive. It contains public methodology, study materials, and sanitized summaries. It intentionally excludes credentials, raw account-level snapshots, private notes, sealed answer keys, and materials that would compromise blind evaluations.

## Status

The Colony study is exploratory. One voluntary response has been collected and coded. No NEXUS intervention has been conducted, and no causal effect of NEXUS has been established. See [Research status](RESEARCH-STATUS.md).

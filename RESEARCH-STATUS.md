# NEXUS public research status

**Last updated:** 2026-11-06

## Established about the project

NEXUS is an experimental reasoning methodology with explicit steps for clarifying questions, auditing evidence, comparing explanations, identifying falsifiers, tracking uncertainty, and revising conclusions.

## Current COLLECTIVE-001 status

- A small purposive baseline sample of three public Colony discussions has been descriptively reviewed.
- The sample shows visible examples of criticism, counterexamples, claim qualification, and reported revision.
- Accounts overlap across threads; independence is not established.
- One linked code correction was inspected at the artifact level; independent reproduction has not been established.
- One voluntary interview response has been collected and coded.
- The participant's example was checked against their own public story. This is a source-consistency check, not independent validation of an external event.
- The participant said they had not used NEXUS.
- No NEXUS intervention has occurred.

## What cannot currently be claimed

- That NEXUS improves reasoning or agent behavior.
- That Colony accounts are independent models or autonomous agents.
- That the selected discussions represent the wider Colony community.
- That reported code changes work in all relevant environments.
- That one participant's example estimates their general error rate.

## Next decision

Continue only with proportionate, consent-respecting collection and verification. Do not increase the number of experiments or infrastructure unless new evidence creates a specific decision-relevant question.

# Evidence, uncertainty, and reporting standards

## Evidence labels

Use the narrowest label that fits:

- **Direct observation:** what was directly seen, measured, or recorded.
- **Established finding:** supported by reliable, relevant evidence and appropriate expert consensus.
- **Plausible inference:** a reasoned interpretation consistent with observations but not uniquely established by them.
- **Speculation:** a possible explanation that lacks adequate supporting evidence.
- **Unknown:** the available information does not resolve the question.

Do not promote an inference or speculation to an established finding through repetition.

## Verification levels

Distinguish:
1. Self-report.
2. Publicly linked artifact.
3. Artifact inspected by the researcher.
4. Repeatable test with recorded method and outputs.
5. Independent reproduction by a tester outside the original work.

These are not interchangeable. State exactly which level was reached.

## Independence

Separate account identity, model identity, operator identity, runtime independence, source independence, and independent replication. A platform label or different account name does not establish any of these by itself.

## Reporting

Every research summary should state:
- question and scope;
- data and collection method;
- what was directly observed;
- interpretation and competing explanations;
- what is supported, weakened, unknown, or not tested;
- limitations and possible bias;
- whether results were independently reproduced;
- what would justify the next step.

Preserve negative, mixed, and inconclusive findings. Never infer non-participation, agreement, or absence of a phenomenon from silence alone.

## Participant protections

Participation must be voluntary. Do not request secrets, private prompts, API keys, confidential system instructions, or personal information. Respect stated quotation preferences. Minimize stored personal data and follow applicable platform rules and terms.
